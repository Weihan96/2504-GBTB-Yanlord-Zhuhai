An icon clad in woven metal

Pieces of art expressing made in Gessi

Created in 2017, the Gessi316 collection is characterised by 'woven metal', which later became the company's stylistic signature. The refined decorations Trame, Intreccio, Meccanica, and Cesello impressed on steel, a noble and precious material, acquire additional value on technical products such as bathroom mixers.

CONCEPT

Timeless patterns

The three-dimensionality and material and tactile properties of the processes encourage exploration of the sensory side. An experience capable of generating well-being and emotions, enhanced by the inimitable textures that adorn the metal with new gleams: Meccanica with an industrial-chic flavor, Intreccio with rhombuses reminiscent of Italian artisanal workmanship, Trame with the extraordinary spiral motif, Cesello synonymous with high jewellery.

Gessi316 for the world's best design projects

Perfect for a wide variety of contexts, from public to residential, to yachting, Gessi316 mixers enhance, with simplicity, the AISI 316 stainless steel with which they are made. A durable material over time, resistant and with high aesthetic value.

Shapes and finishes

Gessi316 mixers, with a cylindrical design, of great visual immediacy and essential refinement, are enhanced by patterns, where craftsmanship and the quality of workmanship are best expressed, revealing the company's maximum specialisation. Contemporary finishes give the metal a unique warmth, enriching the possibilities of customisation. From the most decorative to the minimal, each confirms the beauty, versatility, and charm of the most iconic Gessi collection.
