About Beside

The Beside leather-covered bedside table is a storage element for the bedroom inspired by industrial design, enhanced by Studiopepe’s sophisticated approach. The cylindrical and extra-voluminous silhouette of the coffee table appears light and almost suspended thanks to the detail of the leather base, which slims the design. The leather-covered top is spacious and functional, while the door with the metal rod handle provides access to an internal drawer where you can store books, objects, and mementos.
